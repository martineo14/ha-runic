What the Runic.io integration does

It connects your Runic GPS trackers to Home Assistant in two ways: it reads where each vehicle is, and it passes Runic alerts (started moving, entered a zone, theft…) to your automations within seconds.

From the tracker to your automation

Runic cloud

Your tracker reports in

  • The vehicle's position and moving / stopped state are kept by Runic.
  • An alert rule you created in the Runic app fires, for example “vehicle started moving”.
  • Runic sends that alert to your Home Assistant, signed with your whsec_… secret.
The integration

Checks it, then announces it

  • Rejects anything without a valid signature, or older than 5 minutes.
  • Ignores repeats of an alert it already handled.
  • Fires a runic_event in Home Assistant.
  • Moves the vehicle on the map if the alert carries a position.
Your Home Assistant

You decide what happens

  • An automation triggered by runic_event runs: notify your phone, turn on lights, open the garage.
  • The vehicle shows on the map and in zones like Home.
  • The “moving” sensor can trigger automations too.

Two ways data comes in

Every 5 minutes

Home Assistant asks Runic

It calls GET /vehicles and GET /vehicles/{id} with your API token. For each vehicle it keeps three entities up to date:

device_tracker.<vehicle>: latitude and longitude, so it appears on the map.
sensor.<vehicle>_speed: GPS speed of the last fix, in km/h.
binary_sensor.<vehicle>_moving: on when the last fix is under 15 minutes old and at 5 km/h or more.

Runic's own RUN status only means the tracker is active (heartbeats, ignition), so it isn't used for “moving”.

Works on your local network today, with no internet access to Home Assistant needed.

Seconds after an alert

Runic tells Home Assistant

Runic sends each alert to your webhook URL. This is what triggers automations. It covers 20 alert types, including alert.movement, alert.poi_area (entered or left a zone), alert.theft, alert.tow, alert.speed_limit and alert.low_battery.

Needs Home Assistant reachable from the internet over HTTPS (Nabu Casa, a Cloudflare tunnel…), and an alert rule in the Runic app for each type.

    What version 0.1.0 doesn't do yet
    
    
      
      
    • The “moving” sensor only switches off at the next 5-minute poll. Runic has no “stopped” alert.
      • 
      
    • No sensors yet for battery voltage, fuel or odometer, though Runic's API has them.
      • 
      
    • Alerts can't be picked from a list in the automation editor yet. You write the trigger in YAML, as shown above.
      • 
      
    • The setup asks for a webhook signing secret, even if you only want location polling for now.
      • 
      
    • Vehicles added to your Runic account later only appear after you reload the integration.
